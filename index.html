Doe Valley Printing: Digital Presence Consulting

Net Impact consulting project | Team member | Social media analysis

Our team consulted for Doe Valley Printing, a screen printing and embroidery business in Elizabethton, Tennessee, to improve its website visibility, social media engagement, and reach with target customers such as schools, churches, and local organizations. I analyzed the company's social media data to find gaps in engagement.

  • Found that the website was technically healthy but had a very low SEO visibility score, with only 19 keywords tracked.
  • Recommended using more effective, high-intent SEO keywords and collaborating on posts with other companies.
  • Recommended refining the Instagram page to rely less on AI-generated content.

Management Analysis: Rutgers Entrepreneurial Society

Management Skills course, Rutgers Business School | Fall 2025 | Team member

For a class project, our team analyzed the management challenges of the Rutgers Entrepreneurial Society (RES), including declining attendance at general meetings. We interviewed the club's president, a general member, and an executive board member, and I analyzed attendance trends, such as roughly 150 attendees at Shark Tank compared with about 50 at general meetings.

  • Connected the attendance drop to weak communication and a reliance on extrinsic motivation, such as free food and networking.
  • Recommended offering incentives to bring new members to meetings and communicating more consistently on Instagram.
  • Recommended adding younger members to a senior-heavy executive board.
